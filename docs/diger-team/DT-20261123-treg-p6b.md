## 1. Overview & Architectural Motivation

Building upon Phase 6 Part 1 (which synthesized contracts for all 3 providers and introduced capability tags and the 402 circuit breaker in `965338c9`), Phase 6 Part 2 unifies the planner's brain files:
1. **Removes the isolated web-step wall (`directWebEndpoints`):** The LLM planner reads unified candidate tools across all three live providers (TREG, Firecrawl, Context.dev) purely through declarative capability tags, circuit breaker availability, declared output fields (`item_fields`), reliability metrics (`observed.ok_rate`), and cost per call.
2. **Entity Official Site Reading Widening Proposal:** Extends scope-separated widening (`kind: "entity_website"`, `scope: "widened_entity_site"`, `scope_label_ar: "الموقع الرسمي للجهة"`) when primary account queries finish with shortfall.
3. **Bounded Two-Stage Site-Reading Engine:** Performs bounded `site.map` ($\le 50$ URLs) and bounded `page.read` ($\le 4$ candidate pages), evaluated by the LLM topic judge.
4. **Customer Billing Invariant:** Customers are billed **strictly for delivered matching rows**, never for raw pages crawled or scanned ($0 on zero delivery).
5. **Gateway Budget & Call Caps:** Strictly enforces budget bounds ($0.025 bound check for stealth scrape, max 4 calls per step, max 24 calls per job, 402 breaker open trip with failover to next provider).
6. **Zero Provider Brand Names:** Selection and routing logic in `src/agent/` contain 0 hardcoded occurrences of `"firecrawl"`, `"contextdev"`, or `"treg"`.

## 2. Changes Made (Brain Files)

- Added `siteMap` method supporting both `contextdev.map` and `firecrawl.map`.
