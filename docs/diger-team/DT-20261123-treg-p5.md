## 1. Overview & Scope Separation

### Part A (Implemented in this PR):
1. **Catalog-Wide Harvester & Validator (`scripts/treg/refresh-catalog-contracts.ts`):**
   - Automatically ingests all **3,276 sellable endpoints** from `data/treg/treg_catalog_sellable.json` across **106 platform families**.
   - Enforces deterministic OpenAPI/JSONSchema mapping to `AdapterContract` matching Zod `AdapterContractSchema`.
   - Strictly enforces the **Zero-Guess Invariant**: any parameter or limit not declared in public schema metadata remains strictly `null` (never defaults to 20, 50, etc.).
   - Deterministic classification:
     - `"full"`: `inputs_schema`, `outputs_schema`, page-size parameter, and `max_page_size` are all declared.
     - `"partial"`: inputs and outputs declared, but pagination details or maximum limits are undeclared.
     - `"unknown"`: raw HTTP schema with missing inputs or outputs.

   - `data/treg/adapter_contracts.json`: Synthesized contract repository covering all 3,276 endpoints with canonical schema fingerprints for drift detection.

3. **Data-Driven Contract Preference Ranking (`src/lib/agentMission/sourceRanking.ts`):**
   - Candidate ranking applies data-driven contract preference: `contract_status: "full"` (+10) > `"partial"` (+0) > `"unknown"` (-10).
   - Multi-candidate tie-breaking relies solely on declared contract status, reliability, and unit cost.
   - **Zero Hardcoded Names:** Completely free of provider or platform brand strings (compliant with D-0086 / D-0198).
4. **Universal Test Suite (`tests/catalog-wide-contracts.test.ts`):**
   - Validates all 5 mandatory scenarios: full contract execution with page-size clamping, unknown pagination safe termination (`"المصدر لا يدعم صفحات أكثر"`), catalog deprecation invariant, AST/regex zero-provider-name scanner, and 100% universal Zod schema validity across all 3,276 endpoints.

## 2. Active Control Probe Plan Documentation (§5.3)

In accordance with COMMANDER instructions and Point 5.3 of the plan, the active control probe plan is documented here and **NOT** executed live in automated CI or test runs.

### Operational Context & Rationale
For endpoints where public schemas leave pagination parameters or maximum page sizes `null`, empirical resolution is deferred to a usage-ordered control probe rather than guessing.

### Probe Governance & Safety Constraints
1. **Usage-Driven Queue:** Endpoints are queued for probing strictly based on incoming customer mission demand. Zero speculative probing of idle endpoints.
2. **Minimal Probe Payload:** A probe executes exactly ONE cheap call with minimal limits (`limit=1` or `limit=2`) using a synthetic benign probe query.
3. **Pre-Approved Hard USD Cap:** Monthly probe operational spend is bounded by a strict hard ceiling ($10.00/month), pre-approved by الكبير (COMMANDER).
4. **Strict CI Isolation:** Probes NEVER run in automated test suites or CI/CD pipelines. All test suites mock `fetch` to throw immediately on unmocked network calls.
5. **Financial Separation:** Probe spend is company operational catalog maintenance expense, completely separated from customer-charged mission budgets.
