## 0000000. Public page «خدماتنا» with the full catalog (branch `claude/services-page`, from main `2548785`, 2026-10-24)

- One data module, `src/lib/servicesCatalog.ts` (GENERATED by `scripts/build-services-catalog.ts` from `scripts/catalog/classification.json` and `platforms.json`; do not edit by hand): sector -> platform -> services (Arabic name, group, `core` flag, opaque 7-hex slug). Every count on the page is computed from it (`catalogCounts`).
- Classification of the source's 1,326 lines (English labels never shown; the exact lists are in `docs/services-catalog-classes.md`): `read` and `core` are shown; `write`, `own` (reads that only work on the customer's own account), `private` and `helper` (lookup tables, job status) are not. Platforms that are only the customer's own account (analytics, search console, tag manager, own ad accounts, workspace chat) have no card.
- `/services` is static (`force-static`), platforms collapsed, services rendered only when a platform is opened or a search narrows the list; Arabic search in the browser. The old priced cards stay on the page («خدمات بأسعار معلنة», prices only from the shared pricing functions). Header link «خدماتنا»; on screens under 520 px the header's «الأسعار» link is hidden to make room (it stays in the landing footer and /pricing is untouched).
- Status: every listed service says «اطلبها الآن» (one constant); one honest line from one constant. NOT PROVEN that a service behind a label can be delivered today.
- INTERNAL (never shown): of the 959 shown services, 16 have a real executor in the code registry (hand-mapped from the 21 approved capability slugs: TikTok profile / video comments / video search, Instagram profile / post comments, YouTube video comments / video search, X post search, LinkedIn member profile, Facebook post comments / public post search (also the keyword-comments service), Reddit post comments, company search, people search, work email (multi-provider chain behind `MULTI_PROVIDER_ENABLED`) and decision-maker phone). The other 943 have none in the code; whether the database's capability rows or the live catalog search can plan some of them was not read (no database access). Today, a plan whose items resolve to no known capability is refused with «تعذر مطابقة الخطة مع الخدمة المطلوبة» (`validatePlanPlatformMatch`); the chat does not yet say «سنبحث عن طريقة»: that wording is a follow-up (Part 4 territory), money rules (reserve, release the whole hold when nothing is delivered, charge only delivered units) are unchanged.

## Status of each service (added 2026-10-24, owner decision)
- Two values, derived from ONE constant: «متاح الآن» for the registry slugs in `AVAILABLE_NOW_REGISTRY_SLUGS` (`src/lib/servicesAvailability.ts`: company search, people search, X post search, Facebook keyword comments, Facebook page posts, Facebook post comments, TikTok video comments); «قريبًا» for every other service, including those with a code executor that was not proven live. Flip a service after a live test by adding its registry slug to that list (one line).
- «قريبًا» services show the chip and «ستُضاف تدريجيًا», no call to action, no price. The priced cards on /services are only for available services. Within a sector, platforms with at least one available service come first.
- `docs/FUTURE_SERVICES.md` is generated by `scripts/build-services-catalog.ts` (prerequisites, rollout order, what to measure).
