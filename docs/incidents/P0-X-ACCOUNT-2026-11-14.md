# P0, 14 Nov 2026: X requests that named an account were searched across all of X, delivered and charged as completed

**Status:** fix implemented on branch `ccr-18c58113-f36vic` (PR below). Regression tests use recorded production evidence and make zero paid calls. **No balance, refund, price, ledger or settlement-math change was made.** The customer remediation in section 7 is a **proposal for the owner**: nothing was refunded.

## 1. What the customer asked

400 real X posts from the **Saudi Ministry of Health's official account** about health rumors, debunking or anti-rumor work, preferably within five years.

## 3. The trace (A): request → context → interpretation → plan → query → results → validation → status

1. **Request reading** (`requestContract.ts` `sourceOf`, `topicOf`):
   * `sourceOf` reads a named source only after the words «صفحة/حساب/قناة NAME».
     * «على وزارة الصحة السعودية» (msn_93ed) was read as **no source**.
     * «الصفحة الرسمية لوزارة …» (msn_fefee) was read as `official` with no name.
   * `topicOf` stopped only at «من/في/على/…».
     * «… عبر الصفحة الرسمية … أو أي تصحيح …» became the topic (msn_fefee).
     * «الشائعات فقط» kept «فقط» (msn_dd7c).
2. **Conversation context (B)** (`chatTurn.ts`):
   * The round 57 rule («a follow-up never becomes a new mission of its own words») covers only an **open** request: planning, a question, or a plan waiting for approval.
   * `msn_fefee` had ended `unserved`, so «طيب ابحث عن الشائعات فقط» became a new mission of its four words.
   * The platform, count and account were asked again from scratch, and the original wording and the topic qualifier «الصحية» were lost. (`msn_93ed`'s three-month period was in an earlier conversation and was never carried over either.)
3. **Plan compilation (C, D)** (`compilePlan.ts`, `toolCards/x.ts`):
   * X's only verified parent card, `anyapi.x.search.posts`, has **no account input**.
   * `carriedBy` therefore counts a named source as «not carried», and `parentInputs` never wrote one.
   * The compiler still priced the plan, choosing rows by rank and marking the others as «alternatives».
   * The provider's catalog entry documents `from:` inside the query («operators like from:, since:, until: … work inline in the query»), but the compiler never used it.
4. **Provider query and results:** the queries in the table above. All three were searches across all of X.
5. **Delivery validation (I)** (`foundation/deliveryContract.ts`):
   * The contract, by its own design, **is not a filter**: «it never removes a row, never changes a value, never blocks or delays a delivery».
   * It completed the missing named-source marks (158 completions) and raised an internal admin flag.
   * Nothing carried that finding to the status or to the customer's sentence.
6. **Customer-facing status (G, H):**
   * `status = delivered > 0 ? "completed" : "unserved"`, so both deliveries were labelled `completed`.
   * The reason sentence for the recorded stop `source_exhausted` was «لأن المصدر لم يعد يعطي نتائج أخرى لهذا الطلب».
   * That sentence was false: only an incorrectly broadened query had run out, not the customer's request.

## 4. Root cause

**X had no enforceable account condition.**

* A named account on X was either not read at all, or read and then silently dropped from the query.
* The compiler priced the resulting all-of-X plan instead of refusing it.
* The customer's later turns were not carried over once a request had ended.
* At delivery, a file whose every row was marked «غير مطابق» for the named source was closed as `completed`, with a sentence claiming the customer's request was exhausted.

## 5. The fix (one change, contract fidelity and truthful delivery)

| Area | Change | File |
|---|---|---|
| D/E: enforceable account | A username (given by link, `@`, or as the answer to the question) is written as `from:<username>` in the query (the provider's documented operator). Every row is also checked by its own `username` (**exact**, case-insensitive): a fan account, a similar spelling or a copied display name is never the account. The check runs at planning (`parentEligible`) and on every row read during execution (an `exact` account filter, reason `filter_account`: not delivered, not charged). | `foundation/xAccount.ts`, `foundation/compilePlan.ts`, `filters.ts` |
| E: never guessed | An account named only by its Arabic name is asked **once, before anything is read, held or priced**: the username or the link, or the explicit choice «ابحث في كل إكس بدون تقييد بحساب» (the plan then says the results are not that account's), or cancel. An unusable answer ends the request honestly, with nothing charged. `compilePlan` itself refuses (`x_account_unverified`) to price an X plan whose named account it cannot enforce. | `agent/src/compiled.ts`, `agent/src/runMission.ts`, `agent/src/runState.ts`, `foundation/countChoice.ts` |
| Reading | On X, an institution named after «على/عبر/من/لـ» and «الصفحة/الحساب الرسمي لـ NAME» are read as the named account. The topic stops at «عبر», «لدى», «أو أي» and «فقط»; a trailing restriction word («فقط», «بس», «فحسب», «حصرًا», «لا غير») is never part of the topic. | `requestContract.ts`, `foundation/ask.ts` |
| F: across turns and edits | In a revised request, the **latest change wins for what it names** (topic, count, source, period), and everything else stays the request's own. A short follow-up (a follow-up word or a trailing «فقط», no platform named) after the conversation's latest request **ended** (unserved or completed) is planned from that request plus the change, not from its own words. | `requestContract.ts`, `foundation/ask.ts`, `chatTurn.ts` |
| G: no false «exhausted» | The `source_exhausted` sentence now reads «لأن المصدر لم يعد يعطي نتائج أخرى للبحث المعتمد في خطتك»: the plan's search, never the customer's whole objective. | `agent/src/finalize.ts` |
| H/I: partial and not-the-source is said | When delivered rows are not from the named source (`namedAuthorVerdict` on the delivered rows), the result sentence says so. When none is from it, it also says it is a **partial delivery that does not meet the request as written**. An admin flag `named_source_mismatch` is raised. The settlement math, status values and ledger are unchanged. | `agent/src/finalize.ts`, `agent/src/runMission.ts` (summary text only) |

**Not changed:**

* pricing, reserve, settlement math, ledger, refunds;
* the methodology purchase;
* the four other social pipelines' parsing (the institution reading is X-only);
* any switch or setting.

No provider or model was called.

### What this exact customer case does now

* «أريد ٤٠٠ تغريدة عن الشائعات الصحية على/عبر الصفحة الرسمية لوزارة الصحة السعودية على إكس خلال آخر ٣ أشهر»:
  * The reading is: platform X, 400 tweets, topic «الشائعات الصحية», the three-month period, and named account «وزارة الصحة السعودية» with no username.
  * **The question comes first**, with zero reads and nothing held: the username or link, or «ابحث في كل إكس», or cancel.
  * Code never guesses the username.
* With the username:
  * the query is `"الشائعات الصحية" from:<username> since:… until:… -filter:replies`;
  * only that account's tweets are chosen, delivered and charged;
  * the card says how many were found against 400 **before approval**, when that is measurable.
* «طيب ابحث عن الشائعات فقط» after it ends:
  * it is planned from the same request with the topic «الشائعات»;
  * the account, the period and the count are kept.
* A plan built under the explicit «all of X» choice:
  * its card says the results are not the ministry's;
  * a delivery whose rows are not the named source's says so plainly.

## 7. Proposed customer remediation (NOT executed: owner approval needed)

1. **Credit 91 points**: 41 for the tweets and 50 for the methodology. Use the owner's existing manual adjustment process, with a ledger note naming the three missions and this document. Do not reverse or edit the original rows.
2. **Tell the customer plainly** what happened:
   * the searches were not limited to the ministry's account;
   * the delivered tweets are not the ministry's;
   * the charge is credited back.
3. **Offer a fresh request after the fix is deployed**, once the customer confirms the account's **username** themselves (code will ask; nobody guesses it):
   * period: as they choose (they said up to five years);
   * the card will show, before approval, how many of the account's tweets the search actually found.
4. **Separate owner decision (not built):** hide or warn on the methodology offer for a delivery with `named_source_mismatch` or a delivery-contract R4 flag. That is a purchase-flow change, out of this P0's scope.

## 8. Residual risks

* `from:` is the provider's documented operator, but it has not been verified by a paid call in this round. The exact per-row username check holds whatever the provider answers: a source that ignores `from:` yields fewer rows, never another account's rows.
* A revision typed **after** the account question was answered starts a new mission (round 57 rule). That mission asks the username again; it is never guessed.
* An X request that mentions an institution only as context after «في/على/من» («إضراب في جامعة القاهرة») now gets the account question. The option «ابحث في كل إكس» continues in one click.
* The edited count above the measured pool (msn_93ed: 1 found, 400 approved) is disclosed on the card, but the edit is still allowed. That is a pricing-flow decision for the owner.
