- **OLD** = the hand-coded chat path, then the worker. The old path's own planning model is forbidden in a replay: a case that needs it is reported «asks the old model» and nothing else is known about it.

Tokens are **estimates** (4 characters per token of what was sent and answered, no prompt cache) priced at 0.10 USD in and 0.50 USD out per million tokens.
- The row review of a topic request is stubbed to «match» for every row on both paths, so a delivered count on catalog-example rows shows the plumbing, not relevance.
- Prices are points per delivered record (fractional unit, the hold is `ceil(volume × unit × 1.15)`, the charge is `ceil(delivered × unit)` and never above the hold). Parameters on the new path carry their provenance in brackets; the old path records none.
- **Read the NEW unit prices with this in mind:** the new path prices a record as `cost per call / (rows per call × share of rows that pass the plan's filters)`, both MEASURED on the sample. A catalog-example sample has ONE row per call, so those prices (L2, L3, L4, L5, L6, P8, P9, P10, P13, A) are per-single-row and far above what a live sample of about twenty rows gives. Only L1 and P5 are priced on a real sample (ten live rows, three of them the account's own posts: 0.0005 USD per call / 0.3 passing = 0.9167 points against the old 0.11715, which assumed every row passes).
- A plan card shows up to three masked rows of a REAL sample call; a plan validated on the catalog's example shows none.
