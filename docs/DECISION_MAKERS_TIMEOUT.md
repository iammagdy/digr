## Root cause

1. **We stopped waiting for a call that the source completed and billed.** `tregClient.ts` aborts with `AbortSignal.timeout(options.timeoutMs)` (the people search was given 30 s by `peopleSearchTimeoutMs`, PR #252). The abort is caught as `network_failure` / 503, people calls have `retries = 0`, and the generic sentence was shown for what was one attempt. The abort came BEFORE the response headers: if the headers had arrived, the call would have been `ok` with an unreadable body and the adapter would have said «استجاب المصدر ببيانات غير متوافقة…», not the generic sentence.
2. **Why the routed call is slow: the gateway holds the connection until its own routing loop ends.** The catalog entry `treg.people.search` is `kind: routed`, `async: null`; it tries up to 26 providers one after another and "misses are billed too" for 17 of them (`cost.note`); its own latency is not published (`p50_ms null`). The response is small (3 rows), the call is not streaming and has no async or polling mode (`async: null` on both the routed endpoint and its child). The duration of the routed call itself is NOT measured anywhere we can read.
3. **The direct child is a different kind of call.** `icypeas.people.search` is `kind: data`, `async: null`, "SYNCHRONOUS", observed p50 584 ms and p95 1,122 ms over 1,276,463 calls, 0.00038 USD per row, `pagination.size` (1 to 200, default 100) is the price dial. A production job through it on 2026-10-16 (`job_quote_1e39…`, `icypeas.people.search`, 5 delivered, cost 0.0038) took 8 s from creation to completion.
4. **The gateway offers a re-read.** `integrate.md`: an idempotent replay "reports 0 and puts the first call's charge in `X-Treg-Original-Cost-Micro`"; `GET /calls/{id}/result` returns "the stored response of a successful catalog call"; `GET /calls` lists recent calls. `openapi.json` does not say what a replay does while the first call is still running.
5. **How the gateway maps our flat body to the child's body is not documented** (the dashboard shows only the child's call). The catalog says locations are "best given as alpha-2 country codes (FR, US); a comma narrows them", so `EG` is the documented form; the body that worked for the owner used `"Egypt"`. Poor relevance does not come from one spelling: the direct call with «Egypt» also returned a person in Bahrain, so the source's location filter is loose and the rows must be judged by us.

## What changed

- **The search goes to the direct source** (`PEOPLE_DISCOVERY_ENDPOINT`, `tregAdapter.ts`) with the body `{query: {currentJobTitle, location (alpha-2), keyword}, pagination: {size}}` built from the plan's params (`icypeasPeopleBody`); the plan, the quote, the prices and the settlement are unchanged. Rows asked for: three times the people wanted, at most 11 (11 × 0.00038 = 0.00418 USD, under the unchanged 0.005 per-call ceiling; the per-job 0.04 ceiling is unchanged).
- **A call cut by our own time limit is replayed once with the same idempotency key** (`executeTregCallWithReplay`): the stored answer is read at no charge and the first call's charge is the one recorded (`callCostMicro`, from `X-Treg-Original-Cost-Micro`). The first wait is up to 20 s (the worker deadline decides), the replay runs only if at least 5 s remain after keeping 12 s for the lookups and the job's spend budget allows one more reserved call. The client now flags `timedOut` and reads the original-cost header.
- **A timeout is told honestly:** «تأخر المصدر في الرد ولم يصلنا ردّه، ويمكنك إعادة المحاولة.» (not deterministic: the retry offer stays; the old sentence said «بعد عدة محاولات» for one attempt).
- **Relevance, before any paid lookup and again in the worker's gate:** a person whose own address is in another country is refused (as before); a company address in another country, or a company with none of the requested activity words in its name, industry or description, goes to the review sheet. Neither is looked up, delivered or charged. Lookups stop once the people still needed have a contact value; people who match come first, those with a contact value before those without.
- **No contact value is read from free profile text:** only the source's structured contact fields (and the lookups' answers) fill the work email and the phone; per-field charging is unchanged (`decisionMakerDeliveredUnits`).
- **Decision, for the owner:** for a person the company's declared industry counts as evidence together with its name and description (the company directory gate refuses a label alone). The stricter rule would deliver fewer people.

## Follow-up: the preview live test of 2026-10-26 (job `job_quote_dae643…`, quote 579, hold 666)

The job finished in under a minute, the money was right (hold fully released, 0 charged), and six problems were found. Read-only database reads and code only; no price, SQL, Edge Function or paid call.

1. **Email 0 of 3, phone 0 of 3.** `job_events` 1693 to 1697 and `provider_costs` 1011 (search: 7 units, 0.00342 USD, 7,275 ms) show six lookup calls (one email and one phone per matched person), billed 0 in total. A miss on a per-success provider is free, so «billed 0» is consistent with «no provider found a value» but also with a call that failed before reaching a provider. The per-call status, the endpoint answer and the duration of the lookups are NOT stored anywhere readable (only a console warning on failure), so «not run / deadline / no match / parse failure» cannot be told apart from the database. Not a gate block (the lookups ran). Change: the lookups now send everything known about the person (the profile link AND the company domain with the full name; the phone lookup adds the email found), which is what the lookups' own contract asks for («send everything you know»), so providers that need a domain and a name are no longer left out. Whether that raises the hit rate is NOT verified.
2. **Excel had company columns only.** Cause: the worker took any job whose rows carry a company name for a company search (`isCompanyResearch`), and the normaliser checked «companies» before «people». Now a people job is a people file: `src/lib/peopleColumns.ts` is the one list (name, title, company, country, city, work email + status, phone + status, profile link, company website, industry, company description) read by the workbook and by the results screen, in the same order. The description column is the company's, never the person's biography. The country, city, website, industry and description columns are written only when a person has a value. A value the source marked invalid is written as «غير متاح» (it is not charged either). The committed sample `public/samples/diger-sample-decision-makers.xlsx` was rebuilt with the real generator (a test checks it).
3. **Ernst AI sent to review as «شركة الشخص خارج النطاق الجغرافي المطلوب».** Not reproduced: «Cairo, Egypt», «القاهرة مصر», «Égypte», «Le Caire» and the other spellings all passed the country check before this change. The stored rows live in file storage, not readable here. The most likely cause is that the company's OWN address (not the person's, which the review sheet used to hide) was another country. Changes: the review sheet now shows the person's address and the company's address; a company address that names several places («Cairo, Egypt; Berlin, Germany») is in scope when any part names the requested country; French, Spanish, Italian and German spellings of Egypt (and French names of the other countries the detector knows) are recognised, so a request for another country now sees «Le Caire, Égypte» as Egypt.
4. **Three people with no email and no phone were delivered.** The existing design (`CONTACT_BILLING_NOTE_AR`): a person with no requested contact value shows for review and is not charged. The gate now enforces it after the lookups ran (reason «لم يتوفر لهذا الشخص بريد مهني ولا هاتف مهني»), so such a person is not delivered, not counted, not charged. A person with one value is delivered with the base price and only the fields that exist. The completion sentence counts people and each contact value («سُلّم ٣ أشخاص (المطلوب ٥)، اثنان منهم لهم بريد مهني واثنان منهم لهم هاتف مكتبي»), in the chat card and in the activity feed; the shortfall sentence says how many went to review and why. A request whose people all lack contact values now delivers zero people (no file, nothing charged): the tester should expect that outcome when no lookup finds a value.
5. **Weak industry evidence.** Choice: the source's industry label alone is no longer evidence (the same rule as the company directory). A person is delivered only when the company's own NAME or DESCRIPTION names the requested activity; a label with no such text goes to review. Side effect: fewer people delivered when the source gives a label and no description. A telecom company whose description says «technology» still passes (the word is one of the three requested); a stricter keyword list would be a separate decision.
6. **Customer text:** all new sentences are Arabic, Arabic-Indic digits, no provider name, no USD, no id, no markdown, no emoji (a test checks each); the released hold wording is unchanged.

## Follow-up 2: the lookups that never ran for most people (preview jobs of 2026-10-26 ~13:30 and ~13:43 Cairo)

Read-only diagnosis (four jobs, `provider_costs` 1012 to 1015): each billed exactly 0.00342 USD = 9 rows × 0.00038, so no lookup returned a billed hit; `units` 7, 7, 8, 8 (calls). A local run of the real adapter on realistic leads reproduced the 8 calls: the per-job allowance is 0.04 USD and every call reserved the whole 0.005 cap even when it billed 0, so only the search and about 7 lookups fit, and roughly 3 of 9 people were ever looked up. The domain was also taken blindly from `lastCompanyWebsite` («linktr.ee», «linkedin.com»). What each lookup answered was not stored anywhere readable.

Changes (no price, no SQL, no new table):
1. **Budget:** a call whose cost is reported (a hit, a free miss, a refusal) gives back what it did not spend of its 0.005 reservation (`settleCall`); a call whose cost is unknown (timeout) keeps the whole reservation. What is left is the 0.04 ceiling minus actual spend, so spend cannot pass it; the 0.005 per-call cap header is unchanged. People are looked up best identity first (profile link + real domain), and lookups still stop once the people still needed have a contact value or the worker's time runs out.
2. **Domain:** `src/lib/peopleIdentity.ts`: a link-in-bio, social, messenger or shortener host (and its subdomains) is never sent as the company domain (`NON_COMPANY_HOSTS`); `first_name` and `last_name` are sent with `full_name`.
3. **Log:** one internal `job_events` row per chunk, event type `lookup_log` (hidden from customers like the review rows): a JSON `{step, summary, lookups[]}`; each lookup has `endpoint`, `step` (key), `keys` (NAMES of the identity keys sent), `status`, `latencyMs`, `costUsd`, `servedBy`, `tried`, `outcome` (hit, miss, error, timeout, mismatch), `emailPresent`, `phonePresent`, `calls`, `error` (a short code). No name, email, phone, link or token. Read it with: `select message from job_events where job_id = '<job>' and event_type = 'lookup_log'`.
4. **Name check:** an email returned with a first and last name (or full name) is kept only when it matches the requested person (accents, case, hyphens and spaces ignored); otherwise it is discarded (`mismatch`), not delivered, not charged, and not used for the phone lookup. An answer with no name cannot be compared and is accepted.

Unchanged: prices 83/55/55, per-field charging, a person with neither value goes to review, the Arabic customer text.

## Follow-up 3: the work-email ceiling, the 25 s limit, personal mailboxes, and the price of 120 points for a work email

Evidence (preview `lookup_log` of two jobs, and the owner's controlled measurement through the provider tools). At `X-Treg-Route-Max-Cost` 0.005 every email and phone lookup answered HTTP 200 with cost 0 and found nothing: the gateway tried 23 providers for an email and 15 for a phone and skipped the stronger ones as «would exceed max cost». One email lookup hit our 12 s limit and its replay returned HTTP 409. The owner's measurement (6 Egyptian CEOs, the full identity body): at 0.005, 0 of 6 emails; at 0.02, 6 of 6 (tomba 0.0089 ×3, limadata 0.02 ×2, dropleads 0 ×1; average 0.0111 USD per found address; one of the six was a personal Yahoo address); at 0.03 no more hits. Latencies at 0.02 were 7 to 57 s. Phone cost at a higher ceiling is NOT measured: the phone ceiling stays 0.005.

Owner decisions applied: a work email is 120 points (was 55); base person 83 and phone 55 are unchanged; per person with both fields 83 + 120 + 55 = 258. One price source (`DECISION_MAKER_UNIT_PRICES_POINTS` in `src/functions/shared/decisionMakerPricing.ts`): the quote writer (`planManager`), the calculator, the estimate, the public pages and the assistant read it, and the settlement reads the prices STORED in the quote (`contactUnitPricesPoints`), so no SQL function hard-codes them. The job cap (25,000) and the 15% reserve rule are unchanged: the reserve of 3 people is `ceil(3 × 258 × 1.15)` = 891; 3 people with 3 work emails and 1 phone are charged 3 × 83 + 3 × 120 + 1 × 55 = 664, never above the reserve; a field that is not delivered is not charged.

Changes:
1. **Email ceiling:** `treg.people.email.find` is sent with `X-Treg-Route-Max-Cost` 0.02 (`DECISION_MAKER_EMAIL_ROUTE_COST_CAP_USD`); the search and `treg.people.phone.find` keep 0.005. Every call reserves its OWN ceiling from the job's budget; a call whose cost is reported gives back what it did not spend; a call with no answer yet (our limit, or 409) keeps its reservation. The reads of the same idempotency key (below) reserve nothing more: the gateway never bills one key twice.
2. **Job ceiling:** the job's provider spend is limited to `0.04 + 0.02 × people wanted` (`decisionMakerJobSpendCeilingUsd`): 1 person 0.06, 3 people 0.10, 10 people 0.24 USD. The worker passes `ceiling − spend so far` as the budget of each chunk, and the daily provider cap and the capacity gate use the same worst case (`estimateDecisionMakerChunkSpendUsd` = the ceiling for the people of the chunk; the plan-time gate counts the work email at no less than 0.02).
3. **Limits and reads:** email lookups wait up to 25 s (`PEOPLE_EMAIL_LOOKUP_TIMEOUT_MS`), phone lookups 12 s, both never longer than the worker's deadline (45 s) minus 6 s kept for saving, and none starts with less than 3 s left. A call our limit cut is NOT replayed at once: after 4 s the same idempotency key is read once; HTTP 409 means «still running» (not a failure, no extra charge) and gets one more read after 3 s if the deadline allows; otherwise the lookup is logged as `timeout`. Because a slow email can take 25 s, the people are looked up in groups of at most three at the same time (the emails of a group, then its phones), best identity first, and never more than the people still needed.
4. **Personal mailboxes:** an address on `gmail.com`, `yahoo.com`, `outlook.com`, `hotmail.com`, `live.com`, `icloud.com`, `aol.com`, `proton.me`, `protonmail.com`, `gmx.com`, `mail.com`, `yandex.com` and the like (and regional variants such as `yahoo.co.uk`) is not a work email: it is not delivered, not charged, and logged as `personal_email` with no address. A person with no other contact value goes to review as before. The list is `PERSONAL_EMAIL_DOMAINS` in `src/lib/peopleIdentity.ts`.
5. **Plan card:** the per-person line and the total read the shared price (258); one added sentence says the work email is charged only when delivered.

Not changed: name matching, profile link + domain identity, the link-in-bio filter, `lookup_log` (key NAMES only), per-field charging, the review sheet, the customer text rules. SQL: none applied and none needed (`settle_people_job_atomic` computes `base × basePerson + emails × workEmail + phones × phone` from the quote's own prices; the only hard-coded number in it is the job cap, 25,000 in the proposal `docs/proposals/job-cap-25000/02_…`, 600 in `20261017181000_settle_decision_maker_fields.sql`: a 3-person job holds 891, so the production function must already carry the 25,000 cap, which this change does not verify).

### Changes
1. `lookup_log` gets `rows`: per person `person`, `gate` (match, uncertain, no_match), `reasonCode` (out_of_scope, no_evidence), `hasDomain`, `hasProfileLink`, `identityNull`, `lookedUp` and `finalReason` (contact, out_of_scope, no_evidence, no_contact, identity_null, personal_email, mismatch). Codes and booleans only. Read it with `select message::json->'rows' from job_events where job_id='<job>' and event_type='lookup_log'`.
2. A discarded personal address logs `mailboxDomain` (`gmail.com`, never the address). After a personal address ONE more email lookup is made, without `linkedin_url` (`domain`, `full_name`, `first_name`, `last_name`), under its own step key `people_email_retry_<step>_<index>` (its own idempotency key), only when the person has a company domain and a name, only while the job's budget holds another 0.02 ceiling, and never after any other outcome. ## Follow-up 5: the final fix on PR #253 (email order, mail-server check, deliverability check, technology evidence) and the audit of the request layer

Evidence (the 13:15 UTC preview job on 362cbe8, plus the owner's independent checks of the delivered file): the first email lookup, made with the profile link, returned a PERSONAL gmail address 2 of 2 times (billed 0.0089 each, discarded: 30% of the job's provider cost; 6 of 6 across the last runs); the retry WITHOUT the profile link delivered both emails (limadata.people.email.find.name 0.02, dropleads.people.email.find 0.018). ### Changes (simulation tests only; nothing was run against a provider)
1. **Email order (A1).** The FIRST `treg.people.email.find` is `{domain, full_name, first_name, last_name}` (no `linkedin_url`) whenever the person has a company domain and a name. The profile-link variant (`{linkedin_url, domain, names}`) is used (a) as the first and only lookup when no company domain exists (all there is), or (b) as ONE fallback after the first lookup MISSED (step key `people_email_link_<step>_<index>`, its own idempotency key, the same 0.02 ceiling, only while the job's budget holds it). Never after a personal address, a mismatch, a timeout, `no_mx` or `verify_invalid` (the profile-link finder is the one that returned personal addresses). Log: `attempt` is `domain_and_name`, `profile_link` or `profile_link_fallback`; `summary.linkFallbacks` counts the fallbacks (`personalRetries` is gone). Personal-domain rejection, name matching and the link-in-bio filter are unchanged.
2. **Mail-server check (A2).** `src/lib/server/mailDomainCheck.ts`: a free DNS lookup of the address's DOMAIN (MX; then A/AAAA as the implicit-MX fallback; a null MX counts as none; 2.5 s limit; cached per request). `none` (the domain does not exist or has no MX and no address record) → the address is NOT delivered and NOT charged, no paid check is made, the log entry is `outcome: "no_mx"` with `mailboxDomain` only. `mx`, `a` and `unknown` (the resolver failed) never keep an address out; the code is logged as `mailServer`. Injectable: `setMailDomainResolverForTesting` (the vitest setup file `tests/setup/mailDomain.ts` puts a resolver that answers `mx` in force before every test, so no test touches the network).
3. **Deliverability check (A3).** Each candidate work email (after the DNS check) is sent once to `treg.people.email.verify` with body `{email}` only (public catalog: the routed endpoint takes `email`; answer `{output, raw, _treg}`; children cost 0 to 0.024 USD, the usual one about 0.0004 to 0.0015), `X-Treg-Route-Max-Cost` 0.005 (`DECISION_MAKER_VERIFY_ROUTE_COST_CAP_USD`), its own step key `people_verify_<step>_<index>`, no prefer/exclude header. The verdict is read tolerantly from `output.status | result | deliverability | verdict | state | validity` (or a boolean `valid`/`deliverable`) with `reason` refining a positive one (`emailVerdictOf`). `invalid` → NOT delivered and NOT charged, `outcome: "verify_invalid"` (the verify entry carries `verdict` and the `mailboxDomain`); `valid` → «متحقق»; `risky` and catch-all → «يحتاج مراجعة»; unknown, an error, a timeout or no budget → delivered with «لم يتوفر تأكيد» (the existing helper `arabicVerificationStatus`; `risky` was added to its «يحتاج مراجعة» pattern). The status shows in the workbook and on the results screen through the existing «حالة تحقق البريد المهني» column. The job ceiling now includes it: `0.04 + (0.02 + 0.005) × people` (1 person 0.065, 3 people 0.115, 10 people 0.29 USD).
4. **Technology evidence (A4).** `src/lib/server/technologyEvidence.ts`, used only for a request for technology companies (a keyword or the Arabic sector label carries a technology signal): the company's name or description with ONE clear signal (software, IT, digital, web, apps, cloud, e-commerce, telecom, AI, VR, ERP, programming, «تقنية», «برمجيات», «رقمي», «اتصالات», «بوابة» …), `systems` in a company's NAME only, or a job title, headline or profile text whose segment names a signal AND a firm word («CEO - IT Consultancy & Management Firm», «Founder | web development agency»). Not signals: «development» alone, «systems» in a description, «mobile», «network», «electronic», «automation». The source's industry label is still never evidence (the source filters by it, so every returned row carries it); a person's «IT Manager» or «Head of IT» alone does not make the company a technology company (it works inside a bank or a hospital too). Choice for the consulting-organisation case: eligible, because the title names a FIRM's activity.
5. **Timeouts (A5).** No new live behaviour: a lookup cut by our own limit logs `firstAttempt` apart and reads the same key after the existing 8 s wait; a deadline that allows no read ends it as `timeout` after one call and the next person is still looked up (tested).
6. **Log (A6).** Codes only: new outcomes `no_mx` and `verify_invalid`, new `finalReason` values `no_mx` and `verify_invalid`, `summary.noMx`, `summary.verifyInvalid`, `summary.verifyCalls`, `summary.linkFallbacks`; the mailbox DOMAIN only for a discarded address; never a name, address, link, company domain or token.

Not changed: 83 / 120 / 55, the 25,000 cap, the 15% reserve, per-field charging, a person with no delivered contact value goes to review and is not charged, no SQL, no Edge Function.

### Audit of the request-understanding layer (read-only; no behaviour changed)
The chat builds the people plan in code, not in the model: `chatHandler.ts` (`mapPlanItems`, the `isPeopleCapability` branch) calls `extractRequestIntent` and `peopleParamsFromIntent` (`src/lib/requestIntent.ts`) and then deletes the model's `q`, `company_domain`, `title`, `keywords` and `country` before writing the code's own. `RequestIntent` has no company field, and `peopleParamsFromIntent` itself deletes `q` and `company_domain`. A request that names a company («شركة فودافون مصر») therefore ends as `{title: "CEO", limit: 3, country: "EG"}` (the default title), with no company, no domain and no activity word: the provider body is `{query: {currentJobTitle: {include: ["CEO"]}}, pagination: {size: 9}}`, which returns arbitrary Egyptian CEOs, and the plan card says only «فهمت أنك تريد ٣ من صناع القرار» because `consultantCopy.ts` (`case "people"`) reads only `subject` (the sector). The adapter end already accepts a domain (`icypeasPeopleBody` maps `company_domain` to `currentCompanyWebsite`) and the catalog documents `currentCompanyName`, but nothing upstream supplies either. The model is told the opposite of what the code does: `capabilityCards.ts` lists `company_domain=نطاق شركة محددة إن ذُكرت` and `q=اسم شخص أو شركة` as inputs. Country and sector requests are unaffected (the code builds correct params for them); the named-company case is a code gap, not a model failure. Smallest fix to propose (not applied): add `company: {name, domain?}` to `RequestIntent` (extracted from «في/عند/من شركة …»), pass `currentCompanyName`/`currentCompanyWebsite` in `icypeasPeopleBody`, stop deleting the company in `peopleParamsFromIntent`, add the company to the plan card sentence, judge a named-company row by the company match instead of by activity keywords, and have the model supply the company's English name (the source's data is English).
